# Connoisseur // Manual Test Plan

This is the manual test plan for Connoisseur, the steps to confirm it works before a release is tagged. For what it does, see [README.md](https://github.com/Gogo1951/Connoisseur/blob/main/README.md); for how it works, see [README-Technical.md](https://github.com/Gogo1951/Connoisseur/blob/main/README-Technical.md).

## Before you start

**Run the whole list on each flavor in turn: Classic Era, Season of Discovery on the Classic Era client, WoW Forever, and TBC Anniversary.** Steps are numbered continuously so you can report "failed on step N."

Gather these once so you aren't caught short mid-run:

- **Lua errors on screen.** Type `/console scriptErrors 1`, or install BugSack, so no error slips past unseen.
- **A Mage** that knows at least two ranks of Conjure Water, and **a second player of a lower level** you can target and group with, in steps 2, 10 and 13.
- **A Hunter** that knows Mend Pet, with its pet out and at least one food it eats in the bags.
- **A Warlock** that knows at least two ranks of Create Healthstone and one of Create Soulstone.
- **A Rogue with Poisons trained**, carrying poison, with a poison on its Restock List set above what it carries.
- **A character with at least one unspent talent point**, on every flavor but WoW Forever.
- **Three vendors scouted before you start:** one selling items you can put on your list, one stocking **every** reagent your listed poison needs, and one stocking **only some** of them.
- **Bank access**, with at least one bank bag bought on Classic Era, Season of Discovery and TBC Anniversary, and at least one bank tab bought on WoW Forever.
- **A Hearthstone off cooldown, and a loading screen you can cross**: a boat, a zeppelin, or an instance portal.
- **A game account that has never dragged or resized the Restocker window**, for step 6.
- **Something to fight**, any open-world mob or a target dummy.
- **In your bags:** a buff food and at least two plain foods of different quality, with no Well Fed buff on you, plus a Healthstone and a healing potion.
- **A Russian client** with a Mage and a Hunter, for step 4 (a Korean client serves for the Mage half), and any non-English client for the optional last step.

Unless a step says otherwise, be **out of combat with no target selected**, and untick **Enable Diagnostic Tools** when a step that used it is done: while it's on, every bank restock prints a step-by-step trace to chat.

This plan deliberately skips casting Ritual of Refreshment and Ritual of Souls, Rogue poison hands, Druid DruidMacroHelper wraps, Night Elf stealth eating, Goblin and Gnomish explosives, the Feed Pet quest-food skip, Restock List upgrades on level-up, the List Builder's login offer, the entering-town reminder, per-character settings scope, the Profiles panel, the arena rules, and the API Endpoints, Event Registration, Item Selection and Saved Variables reports in Diagnostic Tools. Refresh coverage there when a release touches those systems.

## Verify this release's changes

Since the last release (2026.09.18.C), Connoisseur ships one TOC per flavor, and each loads only the data built for its own client, so Season of Discovery and WoW Forever now run on data of their own. The release also writes conjure ranks the way each client writes them, stops Feed Pet from reviving a pet that was only dismissed, keeps a Restocker window you opened yourself open until you close it, ends a merchant run cleanly when your bags fill, merges partial stacks in the bank, gathers the Food & Water options with a new Use Conjured Food & Water First, lets the Health Potion macro fall back on Healthstones, fits the Readiness Report to each flavor, keeps WoW Forever from erroring on buffs it hides in combat, and adds the Food & Potion and Water & Potion macros. Run these first, on every flavor.

**One TOC and one data folder per flavor**

**1.** At character select, open the **AddOns** list: **Connoisseur & Restocker** must be listed, enabled and not marked out of date, with its icon beside the name. Log in, open **Options > Connoisseur > Diagnostic Tools**, tick **Enable Diagnostic Tools** and press **Show Connoisseur Context**. The report's first line must end in `Flavor Vanilla // Data Vanilla` on Classic Era, `Flavor Vanilla // Data Discovery` on a Season of Discovery realm, `Flavor Camelot // Data Camelot` on WoW Forever, and `Flavor TBC // Data TBC` on TBC Anniversary. Further down, the 19 **Validate Data** sections must be titled with that same folder, such as **Validate Data: Camelot/Food-and-Water-Camelot.lua** on WoW Forever. Press **Validate Data** in each and let it run until the "Validated N / M IDs" progress line gives way to the report: no row may begin `NOT ON CLIENT`, `TABLE MISSING` or `ERROR`. An `INCOMPLETE` row usually means the client was slow, so run that section again and report any that stay. Failure is the add-on missing from a client's list, the wrong folder on any flavor (Season of Discovery is the one to watch, since it shares Classic Era's client), or a flagged row.

**Conjure ranks**

**2.** *Mage.* Right-Click `- Water`: it must conjure your highest rank of Conjure Water, and the `/cast` line in its body (type `/macro` to read it) must pin that rank the way your spellbook writes it, as in `Conjure Water(Rank 7)`. Target your lower-level second player: the next Right-Click must conjure the rank for their level, and clearing your target must bring back your own. Then Middle-Click `- Water`. On Classic Era, Season of Discovery and WoW Forever, which have no Ritual of Refreshment, it must drink just as a Left-Click does. On TBC Anniversary it belongs to Ritual of Refreshment, which a Mage below level 70 answers with "Connoisseur // You don't currently know Ritual of Refreshment." Failure is nothing conjured, the wrong rank, a `/cast` line with no rank, or a Middle-Click that does nothing.

**Feed Pet**

**3.** *Hunter.* Ctrl + Left-Click `- Feed Pet`: your pet must be dismissed. Target a dead mob and press an attack on it so the game refuses with a red error, then Left-Click `- Feed Pet`: it must call your pet back, never cast Revive Pet. Left-Click again: the pet must eat food from your bags. Right-Click: Mend Pet. Now let your pet die and Left-Click: Revive Pet. Once it stands, the next Left-Click must feed it rather than revive it again. Failure is Revive Pet cast on a pet that was only dismissed, a click that does nothing, or a macro stuck on Revive.

**Rank text and the macro size limit on a Russian client**

**4.** *Russian client.* As a Mage, Right-Click `- Water`: it must conjure, and the rank in its `/cast` line must match, character for character, the rank text the spellbook shows under Conjure Water. A Korean client proves the same thing. Then, as a Hunter, read `- Feed Pet` in the macro window. To stay inside the game's 255-byte macro limit it may drop its Ctrl shortcut (Dismiss Pet) and then its Shift shortcut (Revive Pet), but every line left must be whole. For each shortcut missing from the `/cast` line, hold that key and Left-Click: your pet must eat. A shortcut still in the line must still work. Failure is a Right-Click that conjures nothing, a line cut off partway, or a held key that makes Feed Pet do nothing.

**Restocker window rules**

**5.** Type `/crs` to open the Restocker window yourself. Visit a merchant and close it: the window must stay open. Use your Hearthstone, then cross a loading screen: it must still be open. Close it, and on the **Restocker** page tick **Open at Merchant** and **Open at Bank**. Visit a merchant: the window must open with it and close when you close the merchant. Do the same at the bank. Failure is a window you opened closing on its own, or one a visit opened staying behind after it.

**6.** On the game account that has never dragged or resized the Restocker window, type `/crs`, then `/reload` with the window open: no Lua error may appear, and `/crs` must reopen it at the right edge of your screen. Drag it against the top edge: it must stop there rather than slide off. At the bank, and again at a merchant, click **Delete** in the window's footer: the confirmation must appear in front of the window, readable and clickable. Click **No**. Click the **List** selector to open its menu, then click it again: it must close, and one row's **Rep** cell must do the same. Type `999999` in the add box and press Enter: chat must print "Connoisseur // No item with ID 999999 exists." and no row may appear. Failure is a Lua error at the reload, a window you can push off-screen, a confirmation hidden behind the window, a menu that stays open on the second click, or a blank row.

**Merchant and bank runs**

**7.** Leave exactly one free slot in your bags, and set a Restock List row, with **Buy** on, to more than a stack above what you carry of something your vendor sells. Open the vendor: it must fill the free slot and stop, and chat must end with "Connoisseur // Your bags filled up before everything was bought." Then, on the Rogue, untick **Buy** on your poison's row and open the vendor stocking every reagent it needs: none of them may be bought. Tick **Buy** again afterwards. Failure is a red "Inventory is full" error, nothing bought at all, buying that stops with no line saying why, or reagents bought for a row with Buy off.

**8.** Put a Restock List item into the bank as three small stacks, split with Shift + Click (5, 4 and 2 of one that stacks to 20, for example), with your bags already holding its Amount. On WoW Forever, use a bank tab. Open the bank and let "Restocking complete..." print: the bank must now hold that item in as few stacks as it fits, one stack of 11 in the example. Then pick **New List** from the **List** selector, so this character's list is empty, and open the bank again: nothing may move, no "Restocking complete..." line may print, and the Restocker window must not open by itself even with **Open at Bank** ticked. Switch back to your usual list afterwards. Failure is the small stacks left as they were, or a restock message from an empty list.

**Food & Water options**

**9.** Open **Options > Connoisseur > Macros**. The **Enable Macros** checkboxes must read Bandage, Explosives, Food and so on with no leading "- ", though the macros themselves keep their "- " names. **Food & Water** must sit just above **Potions & Healthstones** and hold **Prioritize Buff Food**, **Enable Scroll Buffs** and **Use Conjured Food & Water First**, a blank line apart. Tick **Enable Scroll Buffs**: the scroll-type checkboxes must appear beneath it, and go again when you untick it. Every dropdown that tunes a checkbox must stay hidden while the checkbox is off and appear on that checkbox's own line, with no caption, once it's ticked: the three in Food & Water, **Re-Apply Expiring Buffs**, **Use Pet Food Buffs**, **Enable DruidMacroHelper Integration** on a Druid, the three reminders on the **Restocker** page, and **Buffs Expiring Within** and **Damaged Gear Below** on the **Readiness Report** page. **Play Sound** must appear beneath **Enable In-Town Restock Reminders** only while that box is ticked. The when-to-use dropdowns (Buff Food, Scroll Buffs and conjured food, plus Pet Food Buffs) must each list **Always**, **When Solo**, **When in a Party or Raid**, **When in a Raid**, **When Leveling** and **When at Max Level**, in that order. Every dropdown on these pages must be the same width, Explosives' **Click Layout** included, which now reads **Left-Click @player** or **Left-Click Toss**. The **Pet Food Buffs** section is flavor-sensitive: it must appear only on TBC Anniversary, its food checkboxes reading **Kibler's Bits** and **Sporeling Snack**, since the other flavors have no pet buff food. Failure is a "- " left on a checkbox, rows packed with no gap, a dropdown showing while its checkbox is off, one on a line of its own or under a caption, a when-to-use list that differs from the others, a dropdown wider or narrower than the rest, or the Pet Food Buffs section on the wrong flavor.

**10.** *Mage below max level, ungrouped.* Under **Diagnostic Tools**, press **Show Connoisseur Context**: its "Max level:" line must read your client's level cap, such as 60 on Classic Era or 70 on TBC Anniversary, never `nil`. **Use Conjured Food & Water First** must start unticked. Carry conjured water plus a vendor water that restores more (conjure for your second player's level to get a lower rank, and bank or destroy any of your own rank): `- Water` must pick the vendor water. Tick the option: its dropdown must appear set to **When Leveling**, and within a second or two `- Water` must switch to the conjured water. Set **When at Max Level**: back to the vendor water. Set **When Solo**: the conjured water again, then group with your second player: the vendor water. Set **Always**: the conjured water, grouped. Untick the option when done. Failure is a wrong or `nil` cap, or `- Water` not following the option, your level or your group.

**Healthstones in the Health Potion macro**

**11.** *Warlock, or anyone carrying a Healthstone.* On the **Macros** page, tick **Combine Healthstones into Health Potion Macro**. With a healing potion and a Healthstone in your bags, `- Health Potion` must list the potion first and the Healthstone's line below it. Now bank the potion: the macro must switch to the Healthstone, and pressing it must use the stone. Failure is "Connoisseur // No suitable Health Potion found in your bags." while you hold a Healthstone, or the Healthstone missing from the body beside a potion.

**Readiness Report per flavor**

**12.** Tick **Enable Readiness Report on Ready Check** and look over the page. **Flask or 2x Elixirs** and **Well Fed (Pet)** must appear only on TBC Anniversary, and **Current Spec** on every flavor but WoW Forever, whose talents work differently. On the character with an unspent talent point, tick **Current Spec** and press **Show Readiness Report** under **Diagnostic Tools**: the Character line must name your biggest talent tree with your point spread, plus "1 Unspent Talent Point" or "N Unspent Talent Points". Then tick **Healing Potion**, add your only healing potion to this character's page of the **Ignore List** (**Add by Item ID**) and press **Show Readiness Report** again: Healing Potion must not appear under "Missing Items :". Take it off the Ignore List afterwards. Failure is a switch on the wrong flavor, a missing spec line, or the report naming a potion you are carrying.

**Buffs hidden in combat on WoW Forever**

**13.** *Grouped with your second player, with the Readiness Report's **Healing Potion** ticked and no healing potion in your bags, and **Prioritize Buff Food** on with Well Fed up.* Pull a mob. While in combat, tab to yourself, to your second player and back, cast a few spells, Right-Click the mini-map button twice, and start a ready check. No Lua error may appear on any flavor. The ready check is where the flavors differ: Classic Era, Season of Discovery and TBC Anniversary must print the report mid-fight, "Missing Items : Healing Potion" included. **WoW Forever is the flavor that breaks here**: its Retail engine can hide buffs during combat, so its report may stay silent mid-fight and speak at the next ready check instead, but it must never error. After the fight, the food you Right-Clicked must be on this character's Ignore List, not taken back off by the second click, and `- Food` must offer your next-best plain food, not your buff food, while Well Fed lasts. Middle-Click the mini-map button afterwards to clear the list. Failure is any Lua error, a silent report mid-fight on Classic Era, Season of Discovery or TBC Anniversary, the food missing from your Ignore List, or `- Food` offering buff food while you are Well Fed.

**Food & Potion and Water & Potion**

**14.** On the **Macros** page, **Food & Potion** and **Water & Potion** must start unticked and sit just after **Food** and **Water** under **Enable Macros**. Tick both: `- Food & Potion` and `- Water & Potion` must appear in the **General** macro tab. With a food, a healing potion and a Healthstone in your bags, read `- Food & Potion`: `[combat]` lines for the same potions `- Health Potion` lists, then one `[nocombat]` line for the food `- Food` picks. Out of combat its button must show the food, and a press eats. Pull a mob: the icon must switch to the potion, and a press uses it. Tick **Combine Healthstones into Health Potion Macro**: the Healthstone's `[combat]` line must appear below the potions, as it does in `- Health Potion`. On a mana user, `- Water & Potion` must do the same with your water and the mana potions `- Mana Potion` lists, with no Mana Gem line. Now bank every healing potion and Healthstone and press `- Food & Potion` mid-fight: chat must print "Connoisseur // No suitable Health Potion found in your bags.", while a press out of combat still eats. Untick both: both macros must disappear. Failure is a combo macro created while unticked, food used in combat or a potion out of it, a combo that disagrees with `- Food`, `- Water` or the potion macros, or a silent press on an empty half.

When steps 1-14 pass on every flavor, this release's changes are verified. Proceed to `4 - Pre-Launch Review Prompt.md`.

## Core checks

**15.** Log in with Connoisseur enabled. No Lua error window and no red text; a colored welcome line must print in the shape "Connoisseur // Version ...". Type `/reload`: the UI must come back clean and the welcome line must print again. Failure is any error naming Connoisseur, or no welcome line.

**16.** Open the options from **every** entry point: type `/foodie`, Shift + Middle-Click the mini-map button, type `/crs config`, and pick Connoisseur from the Blizzard Options > AddOns category list. Each must open the settings **docked inside the Blizzard Options window**, with Connoisseur selected in the category list on the left, showing Macros, Ignore List, Restocker, Readiness Report, Profiles and Diagnostic Tools beneath it. Failure looks like either nothing happening at all, or a standalone window floating free of the Options frame. **TBC Anniversary is the flavor that historically breaks this**, so a tester who runs only Era has not finished.

**17.** Pull a mob. While in combat, try `/foodie`, `/crs config` and Shift + Middle-Click: each must print "Connoisseur // As a safety precaution, the Options Interface cannot be opened during combat." and the panel must **not** open. `/crs` on its own must still open the Restocker window, because only the Options route is gated. Press a Connoisseur macro mid-fight: it must use its item with no error. Now leave combat: the options panel must **not** open by itself, and any macro change that waited on the fight must land within a second or two. Failure is the panel opening, silence instead of the message, a red `ADDON_ACTION_BLOCKED` error, or a macro left stale after the fight.

**18.** Work through the mini-map button's clicks against its tooltip. Left-Click flips Buff Food, Shift + Left-Click flips Scroll Buffs, Right-Click ignores your current food and the **Current Food** row switches to the next best, Middle-Click clears this character's Ignore List instantly with no confirmation (deliberate), and Shift + Middle-Click opens the options. After each toggle the tooltip's Enabled or Disabled must flip while you are still hovering, and must agree with the Macros page. Then untick **Enable Mini-map Button** on the root Connoisseur page: the button must vanish at once, and ticking it must bring it straight back, with no `/reload`. Failure is any click doing something its tooltip doesn't say, the two views disagreeing, or a button that needs a reload to hide or return.

**19.** Type `/crs`: the Restocker window must toggle open and closed. `/crs show` must open it without toggling. `/crs help` must print the show, config and five profile commands, each with a description and no raw keys, the profile lines showing `[name]` where the list name goes. `/crs profile` with nothing after it must print the five profile lines rather than erroring. With the window open, `/crs profile use NoSuchList` must change nothing: the **List** selector keeps your list and every row stays. Failure is a Lua error, a command that silently does nothing, or a blank List selector.

**20.** Loot, buy, or trade yourself a food better than your current pick. Within about a second the `- Food` macro, in the **General** macro tab, must rewrite to the new item, and the tooltip's Current Food row must agree. Now type `/macro` to open the game's macro window and, with it still open, eat or destroy the last of your current best food: `- Food` must **not** change while the window is open, and must rewrite to your next best food within a second of closing it. Then empty every food out of your bags and press `- Food`: chat must print "Connoisseur // No suitable Food found in your bags.", and each macro prints the same shape with its own category name when its category is empty. Failure is a macro that only updates after a `/reload`, a macro still stale after the macro window closes, macros landing in the character-specific tab, silence on an empty category, or the wrong category label.

**21.** *Warlock.* Read the `/cast` lines in the `- Healthstone` and `- Soulstone` macro bodies, then Right-Click each macro. **On Classic Era and Season of Discovery both spells must be bare full names**, such as `Create Healthstone (Minor)`, with no `(Rank N)`. **On TBC Anniversary both must be rank-pinned**, as in `Create Healthstone(Rank 3)`. **On WoW Forever, Create Healthstone must be rank-pinned while Create Soulstone stays bare.** A wrong name makes the Right-Click **silently do nothing**, and Classic Era is where that has broken before, so test the click itself on every flavor rather than only reading the body. Right-Click `- Healthstone` again with the new stone in your bags: it must create a lower-rank backup. Failure is a `(Rank N)` where the flavor casts bare, a missing rank where it pins one, or a Right-Click that creates nothing.

**22.** With **more** of a Restock List item than your target, open a vendor: **nothing may ever be sold**, so check bags and money before and after. With **less** than target and Buy on, open a vendor who stocks it: Connoisseur must buy up to the target and never past it. Hold Shift while opening the vendor: restocking must be skipped entirely. Then, on the Rogue, open the vendor stocking **every** reagent your listed poison needs: each one buys. Open the vendor stocking **only some**: nothing may be bought, and chat prints "Connoisseur // This merchant doesn't stock every ingredient your poisons need. Skipping them all." Failure is any item leaving your bags at a merchant, over-buying, Shift being ignored, or half a recipe filling your bags.

**23.** Put a **Take** item only inside a bank bag, not the bank's main slots (on WoW Forever, inside a bank tab, since its bank has no bags), with your bags short of it, and a **Store** item with more in your bags than its Amount. Hold Shift while opening the bank: nothing may move. Close it and open it again without Shift: the Take item must come out of the bank bag or tab, the Store surplus must go into the bank, and chat must print "Connoisseur // Restocking complete. Hold Shift while opening the bank to skip restocking. Type /crs to edit your Restock List." **WoW Forever is the flavor to watch**, since its bank is built differently. Failure is anything moving on the Shift visit, nothing leaving the bank bag or tab, or a restock with no closing line.

**24.** On the **Restocker** page, set the dropdown beside **Enable At-Merchant Restock Reminders** to **Verbose**, then close a merchant window with something still short. Chat must print the "1 restocking order outstanding." or "N restocking orders outstanding." headline, followed by one line per short item in the shape "3/20 [item]", each a complete line with a working, clickable item link, no `nil`, no stray `%s` or `%d`, and no half-rendered link. Then cross a loading screen without visiting a merchant or the bank: that headline must not print as the world loads, and arriving at an inn or a city may print only the in-town reminder, "Don't forget to restock while you're in town!". Failure is per-item lines missing in Verbose, a broken link, or the outstanding-orders headline turning up on arrival from a boat.

**25.** Open **Diagnostic Tools** on a fresh login: the **Enable Diagnostic Tools** toggle must be **off**, and off again after a `/reload`, since it is deliberately session-only. Tick it, click **Start Event Log**, spam a red combat error by pressing an ability that isn't ready over and over, then click **Show Captured Events**: the spam must **not** appear as individual timestamped lines. Read the `-- Suppressed (uncorrelated) traffic --` block at the end, where it must fold into one counted row such as `x12`. Failure is diagnostics surviving a reload, or spam flooding the log line by line.

**26.** *Optional, non-English client.* Read the options pages, the mini-map tooltip, the Restocker window and the List Builder (the Restocker window's **Open List Builder** button). Every label and tooltip must render in that language with no raw key like `OPTIONS_FOOD_WATER_HEADER` on screen. This release's copy needs the closest look: the Food & Water section and its three options, the six when-to-use choices, the renamed **Add Runes & Other Mana Items to Mana Gem Macro**, the **Food & Potion** and **Water & Potion** checkboxes, the hover text on every Readiness Report switch, and the Restocker window's Copy, Delete and Remove tooltips. The Rogue's poison dropdowns, the pet food checkboxes on TBC Anniversary, and the List Builder's poison and reagent rows now take their names from the game, so each must read exactly as that item does in your bags. The **Buffs Expiring Within** choice of two and a half minutes must use that language's decimal mark, such as "2,5" in German. No checkbox label beside a dropdown may be cut short, no dropdown choice may overflow its box, and no Restocker column heading may grow so wide that it crushes the item name beside it. Trigger a few chat lines, the welcome line, a "No suitable ... found" line, the item-ID line from step 6, the bags-full line from step 7, the poison-reagent skip line from step 22, and a Readiness Report line: each must read as one complete sentence with no `nil` and no stray `%s` or `%d`, and a Readiness Report line must separate its items with that language's own punctuation. The slash commands `/foodie` and `/crs`, the /Commands heading, the name Restocker, the four URLs, the names Discord, GitHub, CurseForge and Wago, and the whole Diagnostic Tools page stay **English on every client**. That is deliberate, not a missed translation.

When every step passes on each of Classic Era, Season of Discovery, WoW Forever and TBC Anniversary, manual testing is complete. Proceed to `4 - Pre-Launch Review Prompt.md`.
