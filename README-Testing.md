# Connoisseur & Restocker // Test Plan

Connoisseur & Restocker keeps your food, water, potion and class macros on your best items, and its Restocker buys and banks your consumables for you. This time we most want you to try the new chat lines that say what the add-on just did, the new **Ignore List** buttons, and the reworked item tooltip counts.

**Play on Classic Era, Season of Discovery, WoW Forever, and TBC Anniversary.** If something looks wrong, tell us the check number and what you saw on [Discord](https://discord.gg/eh8hKq992Q).

## What's New

1. On the **Macros** page of the options, untick a macro under **Which Macros to Create** and tick it again: chat should say where your macros are, and **Open Macros Window** should show them.
2. Right-Click the mini-map button to ignore your best food, then Middle-Click it to clear the list: chat should say what each click did.
3. On the **Ignore List** page, add something with **Add from Bags**, then empty the list with **Clear List**.
4. As a Mage with no water, press `- Water`, then Right-Click it as chat suggests to conjure more; as a Warlock, do the same with `- Healthstone`.
5. Hover over a consumable two of your characters carry: the tooltip should list each character with its bag and bank counts, you on top, and the total at the top right.
6. On TBC Anniversary, as a Warlock with a demon out, tick **Use Pet Food Buffs** and carry Kibler's Bits: `- Food` should feed your demon first.
7. Type `/crs`, click **New List**, then press Escape: the new list should disappear and leave you on your old one.
8. Visit your bank twice in a row: on the second visit, with nothing to move, chat should stay quiet.

## Everything Else

9. Carry a few foods and drinks of different quality and press `- Food` and `- Water`: each should use your best one and follow along as your bags change.
10. Type `/crs`, click **Pick Staples** and tick a few, then visit a vendor and your bank: Connoisseur & Restocker should buy and bank up to each Keep amount.
11. As a Hunter, Left-Click `- Feed Pet` to call, feed or revive your pet, and Right-Click it for Mend Pet, in combat too on WoW Forever.
12. As a Rogue, pick each hand's poison on the **Class & Race** page, then Left-Click and Right-Click `- Poisons`.
13. Turn on the Readiness Report and start a ready check mid-fight: on WoW Forever it may stay quiet until the next one, but it should never show an error.
14. Open the options with `/foodie` and with Shift + Middle-Click on the mini-map button, then try both in combat, where chat should say they can't open.
