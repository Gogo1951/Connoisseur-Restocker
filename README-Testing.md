# Connoisseur // Test Plan

Connoisseur keeps your food, water, potion and class macros on your best items, and its Restocker buys and banks your consumables for you. This time we most want you to try the new **Class & Race** and **Feedback & Support** pages, and Restocker runs at vendors and your bank.

**Play on Classic Era, Season of Discovery, WoW Forever, and TBC Anniversary.** If something looks wrong, tell us the check number and what you saw on [Discord](https://discord.gg/eh8hKq992Q).

## What's New

1. On any character, open **Macros**, then **Class & Race**, in the Connoisseur options: the Druid, Rogue, Forsaken and Night Elf sections should all be there.
2. Open the **Feedback & Support** page in the Connoisseur options and copy one of its links.
3. On WoW Forever, tick **Enable Scroll Buffs**, carry two scrolls whose buffs you're missing, and press `- Food`: each press should use one scroll, then eat.
4. On Classic Era or TBC Anniversary, collapse Secondary Skills on your Skills page, then bandage in a fight until First Aid goes up: `- Bandage` should still use your best bandage afterwards.
5. As a Rogue, put a poison on your Restock List and visit a vendor that sells all its ingredients: Connoisseur should buy them.
6. Fill your bank's regular slots, leave a profession bag in it empty, and visit the bank with a Restock List item to stash: chat should say your bank is full.
7. Right after logging in, type `/crs`: every item should sit under its proper category, not **Other**.
8. On Season of Discovery, carry a food or drink only that season has and check that `- Food` or `- Water` uses it.

## Everything Else

9. Carry a few foods and drinks of different quality and press `- Food` and `- Water`: each should use your best one and follow along as your bags change.
10. Type `/crs`, click **Pick Staples** and tick a few, then visit a vendor and your bank: Connoisseur should buy and bank up to each Keep amount.
11. As a Mage, Right-Click `- Food` or `- Water` to conjure more; as a Warlock, Right-Click `- Healthstone` to create one.
12. As a Hunter, Left-Click `- Feed Pet` to call, feed or revive your pet, and Right-Click it for Mend Pet, in combat too on WoW Forever.
13. As a Rogue, pick each hand's poison on the **Class & Race** page, then Left-Click and Right-Click `- Poisons`.
14. Turn on the Readiness Report and start a ready check mid-fight: on WoW Forever it may stay quiet until the next one, but it should never show an error.
15. Open the options with `/foodie` and with Shift + Middle-Click on the mini-map button, then try both in combat, where chat should say they can't open.
