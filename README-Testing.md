# Connoisseur // Test Plan

Connoisseur keeps your food, water, potion and class macros on your best items, and its Restocker buys and banks your consumables for you. This time we most want you to try the new on/off switches under **Features**, the mini-map button's new clicks, and Cannibalize on a Forsaken.

**Play on Classic Era, Season of Discovery, WoW Forever, and TBC Anniversary.** If something looks wrong, tell us the check number and what you saw on [Discord](https://discord.gg/eh8hKq992Q).

## What's New

1. Left-Click the mini-map button to turn Buff Food and Scroll Buffs off and on together, and Shift + Left-Click it to do the same for the Readiness Report.
2. Untick **Enable Restocker** under **Features** on the Connoisseur options page, then visit a vendor and type `/crs`: nothing should buy, and chat should say the Restocker is off.
3. Untick **Enable Macro Updates** under **Features**, then loot a better food: `- Food` should stay as it was until you tick it again.
4. As a Forsaken, tick **Use Cannibalize Instead of Eating**, then target a Humanoid or Undead you've killed and press `- Food` (on WoW Forever, try **Use Cannibalize Instead of Drinking** with `- Water` too).
5. On any character, open the **Macros** page and check that the Druid, Rogue, Forsaken and Night Elf sections all show, and that a Night Elf Rogue can now use **Enable Stealth Drinking**.
6. On Season of Discovery, carry a food or drink only that season has and check that `- Food` or `- Water` uses it.

## Everything Else

7. Carry a few foods and drinks of different quality and press `- Food` and `- Water`: each should use your best one and follow along as your bags change.
8. Type `/crs`, click **Pick Staples** and tick a few, then visit a vendor and your bank: Connoisseur should buy and bank up to each Keep amount.
9. As a Mage, Right-Click `- Food` or `- Water` to conjure more; as a Warlock, Right-Click `- Healthstone` to create one.
10. As a Hunter, Left-Click `- Feed Pet` to call, feed or revive your pet, and Right-Click it for Mend Pet, in combat too on WoW Forever.
11. As a Rogue, pick each hand's poison on the **Macros** page, then Left-Click and Right-Click `- Poisons`.
12. Turn on the Readiness Report and start a ready check mid-fight: on WoW Forever it may stay quiet until the next one, but it should never show an error.
13. Open the options with `/foodie` and with Shift + Middle-Click on the mini-map button, then try both in combat, where chat should say they can't open.
