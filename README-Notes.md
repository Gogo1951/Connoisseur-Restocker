# Connoisseur // Notes

> The maintainer's settled rulings for Connoisseur, kept so no review raises them again: exceptions to the Gogo1951 add-on Style Guide, and decisions it leaves open.

## Exceptions

- Every character has an AceDB profile of its own (`AceDB:New` gets no third argument), named as AceDB names the character: its first name and surname on WoW Forever, `Name - Realm` on Classic Era and TBC. Scrolls, buff food and the rest of the Macros settings differ by character, so they cannot all sit on one shared profile. This is the Style Guide's Per-Character model. Two characters share a setup only when the player puts both on one profile. The maintainer ruled on 2026-09-26 that every character shares `Default`, and reversed it on 2026-09-30 before any release carried it; releases before 2026-07-25 shared `Default` too, and a migration moves those characters onto their own profiles.
- The mini-map button's position, whether it shows, and the welcome message are global: one choice for the account, never a profile's.

### Profiles panel without suggested profiles (remove after 2026-10-30)

- **Departs from:** OPTIONS PANEL → Profiles Panel, "returns the stock AceDBOptions-3.0 table unmodified".
- **Instead:** The Profiles panel is built with AceDBOptions' `noDefaultProfiles` flag, so its pickers list only profiles that exist, never the suggested Default, realm and class profiles.
- **Why:** While the migration that moves characters off the shared Default profile runs, a character put on Default is moved off it again at its next login.

## Decisions

- The TOC `## Title`, `README.md`'s H1 and the header of the Inventory Report block on item tooltips are `Connoisseur & Restocker`, for discoverability of the absorbed Restocker feature; every other surface says `Connoisseur`.
- The Ignore List is per character: an item ignored on one character is not ignored on another, because the list lives on the character's own profile. The Global list sits outside every profile and is ignored for every character.
- The mini-map button's Middle-Click clears the current character's Ignore List instantly, with no confirmation, as a deliberate quick-clear, and the Ignore List panel ships no clear-all of its own.
- Enable Inventory Report in Item Tooltips sits on the Restocker options page under an Inventory Report header, not on the root Connoisseur page.
- Every row of the Inventory Report block sits one space in from its header, the pad Water Dispenser's item-tooltip block uses, so the rows read as the header's own. One space, not two.
- The mini-map tooltip reads, top to bottom: Current Food, Buff Food, Scroll Buffs, Restocker List, the class notes, the Restocker Report, the Ignore List, then Options. Current Food leads because the button's icon shows that item, and the Restocker Report sits under the class notes.
- The Restocker List has a tooltip block of its own, titled Restocker List, with a line saying what it is and its click beside Open. It is never a row of the Restocker Report: it is a feature of the button, and a report must not hide it.
- A lone item in the tooltip sits right-aligned on the row under its title. A Hunter's Current Pet Food and a Rogue's Main Hand Poison and Off-Hand Poison open their class notes that way, two lines each; Off-Hand carries its hyphen in that title, while the macro rows and the options page say Off Hand.
- Class notes in the tooltip are one row per click, the click on the left and what it does on the right, under the macro's name. They wear the palette's HELP silver, so INFO blue marks only a click on the mini-map button itself.
- The tooltip's Ignore List shows eight items at most and a count past that, the Restocker Report's own limit, so neither list can outgrow the screen.
- A Buff Food or Scroll Buffs switch that is on while its when-to-use choice does not apply shows that choice in gray in the tooltip, in place of Enabled.
- `README.md`'s Appreciation & History thanks only targon (Free Refills), kvakvs and guardycmw, under the 🚀 lead line: Connoisseur is Gogo1951's own, Restocker is an absorbed subsystem, and ChiliFajita's Auto Restocker is gone from CurseForge and GitHub. The in-game Restocker Praise string names ChiliFajita, kvakvs and guardycmw.
- `README.md`'s Related Add-ons files an add-on built around one Connoisseur job, such as Poisoner for poisons or Feed Me for pet feeding, under 🔴 Alternatives.
- Restock Lists are global: `ns.db.global.restocker` holds them, shared by every character and outside AceDB profiles, so a profile switch or reset never touches a Restock List or re-offers the Starter List. Each profile only picks which list it uses, so a character shops from its own list and two characters on one profile share theirs. Resetting a profile keeps the list it was using.
- Files inside a `Features/` feature subfolder carry the feature name in the basename (`Features/Restocker/Restocker-Window.lua`); `Features/Macros/` keeps its own file names.
- Every `ns` function is dot-defined (nothing on the namespace takes `self`), including where guide snippets show colon methods.
- The Readiness Report ships default-off behind `readinessReportEnabled` as a beta soft launch, and stays default-off; the original `readinessReport` key is retired.
- The Restock List window's Keep box (its Amount box, before the rename) saves as you type: closing the window without pressing Enter keeps what the box holds, and an emptied box lands at 0 (store-all for bank deposits). The live write stays. The tooltip no longer says "Press Enter when finished editing.": nothing has to be pressed, and it was one more line to translate.
- Restock Lists are called "List" in every player-facing string, never "Profile"; the `RESTOCKER_PROFILE_*` key names and the `/crs profile` command literal keep their old spelling, and the values are never changed to match the key names.
- New Restock Lists are named for the character's localized class, numbered "Class (2)" on collision, and never auto-join an existing list; legacy "Name-Realm" lists stay as they are, and there is no eponymous backstop list.
- Deleting the Restock List in use moves the character to the top list in the List selector, another character's included, and makes a new empty class-named list only when the deleted list was the last one. A delete never makes a list while another exists: it used to start a fresh class-named list every time, so deleting "Druid (2)" made "Druid (3)" and deleting that made "Druid (2)" again.
- A WoW Forever character is kept and shown by its first name and surname, wherever the add-on saves a character or names one: a Restock List's characters, the Inventory Report, the character's own profile. Classic Era and TBC characters keep their one name.
- The characters on a Restock List are named in their class colors: on the window's Used by line, in the List selector's menu and in the delete confirmation. A character whose class is not known yet, because it has not logged in since the add-on began keeping it, is left uncolored.
- The Restock List window's second row runs, left to right: the filter, Add Item from Bags, the add box with its Add button, then Pick Staples. Add Item from Bags lists every item in the bags that is not already on the list, and picking one adds it. Its caption is drawn in the grey of the two hints beside it, and it stands as far from the add box, as drawn, as Add stands from Pick Staples.
- Red text is for errors alone. A Keep amount the bags are short of is yellow, and the orders count under the list is drawn in the same yellow, which is what tells a player what a yellow Keep number means. The add box's notice, which is an error, stays red.
- Whatever a click acts on in the Restock List window is drawn as a button or a field, never as a colored word: Undo, beside the removal it takes back, is a button. Blue marks information, and a blue word does not look clickable.
- The list bar's button reads Manage Lists, not Manage.
- An item named in the add-on's own windows, panels and tooltips is its icon and its name in its quality color, with no square brackets: the mini-map tooltip, the Restock List window's orders tooltip, removal line and bag menu, the staples tooltips, the Ignore List and the Macros panel. The icon does the brackets' job. Chat lines keep the game's bracketed links, which can be clicked there.
- The Poisons macro keeps its `/click StaticPopup1Button1` replace confirmation even though it also accepts any other confirmation box on top; a Weapon Buff macro may reuse it.
- The Restock List window takes dragged items, on its add box or dropped anywhere else on it, and typed item IDs only: both clients route Shift + Click through `ChatFrameUtil.InsertLink`, which an add-on can't claim without overwriting a Blizzard function.
- The Restock List window keeps its 870px default width, leaving item names 215px at English widths now that the Keep box is narrower than the Amount box it replaced; the column-layout test's default-size floor is 215px to match, and the minimum width (819px) holds the 150px floor with 14px to spare.
- The staples pop-up (the List Builder, before the rename) names its poison and reagent rows with the client's own item names, not translated strings: a poison type by its ladder's first item, any other row by the item a tick adds. Only rows that stand for a kind of item (the food diets, water, ammo) keep a label, and poison-group and pet-food names in the Macros panel come from the client the same way. Its rows live once, in `Data/Data.lua`, never in a copy per flavor folder, and its window is 690px wide so the longest English name, Rune of Teleportation, fits on one line beside its icon.
- A checkbox that stands for an item or a macro carries its icon between the box and the name, a space off the name: the staples pop-up's rows, and the Macros panel's Enable Macros rows, which wear each macro's default icon.
- The staples pop-up has a solid background, so the Restock List it opens over never shows through it. Every staple carries its item's icon between its checkbox and its name, a space off the name; one grid of checkboxes and dropdowns runs through every section; and Water sits in the food grid, not on a row of its own.
- The consumable data tables leave out oddball items that a data check turns up, even when they share a table's cooldown: Scorpid Surprise (5473), Green Tea Leaf (1401), Holy Spring Water (737) and Night Dragon's Breath (11952) stay out.
- Season of Discovery items live only in `Data/Discovery/`: every other data folder leaves them out with a deliberately-absent note, even where its client's Validate Data reports them OK, since OK only proves the client knows the ID.
- A Classic Era Validate Data report also prunes `Data/Discovery/`: Season of Discovery runs on the same client, so a row Era flags NOT ON CLIENT leaves Discovery too, while Discovery's values wait for a Season of Discovery export.
- The Readiness Report's Flask or 2x Elixirs check counts the zone-limited flask and elixir auras (the Unstable and Shattrath flasks, and Bloodberry) as flasked everywhere, because those auras persist outside their zones and the check errs toward silence.
- Star's Tears and Star's Lament are offered only inside an arena (arena column 1), though the client lets them be used anywhere: they are expensive, and ordinary food and water serve better outside one.
- The four Ogre Brews keep Gruul's Lair (330) among their allowed zones: they work there, though their tooltip names only the Blade's Edge Plateaus.
- Harvest Bread and Winter Veil Loaf carry no arena value though their tooltips say Conjured Item: the TBC client doesn't flag them usable in arenas, so they are never offered there. The data's arena column is also the only test for conjured, so Use Conjured Food & Water First treats them as ordinary food, as it does anything that merely sells for nothing (the Underspore Pod, holiday treats).
- The Feed Pet macro's "You don't currently know Mend Pet." stays its own locale string (`TIP_PET_NO_MEND`) rather than joining the spell-name tips built on `TIP_DONT_KNOW_SPELL`: joining would put Mend Pet's spell ID in every data folder, and the only locale whose clients name the spell differently is esES, where TBC Anniversary says "Aliviar mascota".
- Where one language's clients name the same spell, item or interface label differently, its locale file uses the Classic Era and WoW Forever name: one string serves all three clients, and those two agree.
- Use Conjured Food & Water First (Food and Water only, off by default, When Leveling by default) is the only way a macro passes over your best item, for the free conjured food and water a Mage hands out. Potions, pet food and every other macro always use the best, and there are no custom sort orders or second-best rules (issue #40).
- The options panel shows macro names without their leading "- " (the Enable Macros checkboxes read Food, Water and so on); the macros themselves keep it.
- On every options page, a sub-option stays hidden until its checkbox is ticked. A dropdown then appears on the checkbox's own line with no caption, and a break follows every row; only dependent checkboxes (the scroll and pet food types, the in-town reminder's Play Sound) appear beneath their toggle instead.
- Buff Food, Scroll Buffs and Use Conjured Food & Water First share one Food & Water section, each described in its own hover text; Buff Re-Application and Pet Food Buffs keep their own sections.
- Every when-to-use dropdown (Buff Food, Scroll Buffs, Pet Food Buffs, conjured food and water) offers the same six choices in the same order: Always, When Solo, When in a Party or Raid, When in a Raid, When Leveling, When at Max Level. None is trimmed per feature, even where one reads oddly, such as buff food only when solo. Leveling means below the client's own max level, read from the game rather than hard-coded, and one choice covers one condition: group and level never combine.
- Every options dropdown is the same width. A choice too long for it gets shorter copy rather than a wider box: the Explosives layouts name only the Left-Click, since the Right-Click always does the other.
- A Restock List row's Buy toggle governs every purchase the row makes, so a poison row with Buy off has none of its ingredients bought either.
- The Readiness Report counts an item on either Ignore List as carried: it never names something the player has in their bags as missing.
- The Restock window closes with a merchant or bank only when that visit opened it. A window the player opened stays open until they close it, and a loading screen never closes it.
- The pet-food tables leave out foods Feed Pet would waste, even when the client gives them a pet diet: special foods that sell for nothing (Whipper Root Tuber, Essence Mango, holiday foods) and battleground or zone rations. Feed Pet feeds the cheapest food in the pet's level bracket first, so it would spend those before ordinary food.
- The Potions & Healthstones option that eats and drinks out of combat is labeled Use Food & Water in Potion Macros Out of Combat, in the Use wording of its neighbors (Use Conjured Food & Water First, Use Pet Food Buffs).
- The Gold Reserve ships on at 1 gold and applies at every level: a character with less than the reserve buys nothing at a merchant and is told restocking paused, until it has more.
- The Gold Reserve is account-wide, kept with the Restock Lists outside every profile: one floor for every character, so a reserve set on one character also holds back an alt with less gold.
- The class notes in the mini-map tooltip, the Macros panel's class and race sections, and the staples pop-up's Poisons note are headed with the client's own name for the player's class or race, in the singular and with no "Attention" lead: the client has no plural names, and game names come from the client.
